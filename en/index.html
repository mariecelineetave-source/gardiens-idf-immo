What you should know

The three questions everyone asks

Am I allowed to do this?

Yes, on one condition: stay in your lane. French law reserves the sale of property to licensed professionals. You simply pass on information — that is the role of a business introducer, set out in an agreement we sign together.

Does my name come up?

Only if you decide so. When you share, you choose: either we name you to the owner, or we never say your name at all. You decide, opportunity by opportunity.

What if the sale falls through?

You lose nothing, you pay nothing, you owe nothing. The bonus rewards a sale that actually completes. If the project goes nowhere, you are told, and that is the end of it.
